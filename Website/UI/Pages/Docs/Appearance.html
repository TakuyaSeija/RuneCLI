Color theme

Settings → Appearance → Color theme offers Violet, Cyan and Amber. Left/Right on the row switches and saves; Enter opens the separate list, where Enter applies the selected theme. Esc leaves without applying an item you only browsed. The factory theme is Amber.

Theme is presentation, not model behavior or permission. /theme violet, /theme cyan and /theme amber also select a theme. The global key is ui.theme; reset removes your override.

Conversation side panel

Toggle the panel in Appearance, with Ctrl+O or /panel. Its factory preference is enabled. Narrow terminals can hide the side-by-side panel even with that preference on. Hiding it does not stop usage accounting, file tracking or plans.

What the terminal owns

Your terminal/desktop controls wallpaper, transparency, blur, window decorations and font rendering. Rune does not have a background-image or blur setting. The same theme can look different against a light or dark terminal background.

Modified keys and mouse gestures also depend on the terminal. A profile may reserve Ctrl+wheel for zoom, Alt+wheel for horizontal scrolling, or some shortcuts for its own actions.

Advanced display choices

Some color, mouse, scrolling and render-timing values are TOML-only options, not extra controls on the Appearance page. Inspect the specific documented key before changing it. Increasing refresh frequency can consume more resources; a scrolling preference cannot make a terminal report unsupported input.

rune -config explain ui.theme
rune -config reset ui.theme

Reset is a change to the global preference. Use /help for reading and navigation keys rather than guessing from another terminal application.
